## The problem

Public tenders and terms of reference arrive from many donors in different formats. Screening them meant reading long documents, pulling out requirements, and checking each one against technical, financial and eligibility criteria — slow work that was hard to keep consistent from week to week.

## What I built

I systematised the screening into a repeatable workflow: reading tender documents, extracting requirements, applying explicit eligibility criteria, and tracking opportunities — with AI assisting the first-pass analysis and a person making the call.
