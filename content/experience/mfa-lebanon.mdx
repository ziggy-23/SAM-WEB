## Overview

From January to June 2024 I worked as an International Affairs Associate at Lebanon's Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Beirut, on a project about the Lebanese diaspora.

## What I did

- Coordinated the diaspora project across administrative units, embassies and international bodies.
- Merged, cleaned and analysed high-volume diaspora datasets in Stata (do-files, OLS regressions) to provide evidence for a national strategy.
- Structured research-style data repositories to strengthen the ministry's ties with diaspora networks.
