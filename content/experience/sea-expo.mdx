## Overview

The Saudi Entertainment & Amusement (SEA) Expo is one of the region's major industry exhibitions, bringing together companies, investors, developers, manufacturers and institutions from across the Gulf and the wider Middle East and North Africa. I took part in both the 2024 and 2025 editions.

## What I did

I engaged with exhibitors and visitors, identified potential clients, discussed commercial opportunities and facilitated conversations around ongoing and prospective projects — acting as the main point of contact for more than 25 corporate accounts. Along the way I met stakeholders connected to Saudi Arabia's investment and development ecosystem, and heard first-hand how Vision 2030 shapes the Kingdom's ambitions.

Behind the stands and commercial discussions were much larger questions: investment, infrastructure, tourism, urban development, and the creation of entirely new economic sectors.

<Learned quote="A conversation can be just as important as a presentation.">

Riyadh changed the way I look at cities, scale and development. It seems to stretch endlessly — vast distances, constant traffic, thousands of people moving in every direction — and yet it is also a city looking remarkably far into the future.

What stayed with me just as much was the people. I was consistently struck by the warmth, generosity and hospitality I encountered, and by the importance placed on welcoming others, showing gratitude and maintaining relationships.

Professionally, I learned that in the Gulf, commercial relationships are not built only around products, prices or contracts. Trust, patience, personal interaction and an understanding of local business culture matter enormously.

Taking part in two editions also made Vision 2030 tangible: something more than a policy document — a set of real projects, investment discussions and personal ambitions. I left with a much greater appreciation for the region, and the sense of having witnessed a transformation while it was still taking place.
