## Overview

The Saudi Entertainment & Amusement (SEA) Expo is a large-scale industry exhibition in Riyadh. I worked there as a Client Engagement Associate from April 2024 to May 2025.

## What I did

- **Client support and issue resolution.** I was the main point of contact for 25+ corporate accounts across the Middle East, Europe and the US, resolving operational issues and information gaps under tight deadlines, coordinating technical, commercial and legal teams, and reporting status to management.
- **Onboarding and process management.** I coordinated client onboarding, validated contractual and operational documentation, followed up on open actions, and identified process inefficiencies to reduce friction between clients and internal teams.
- **Cross-functional delivery.** I managed many client requirements at once, keeping information accurate between clients and internal teams.
