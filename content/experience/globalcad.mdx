## Overview

GlobalCAD — the Centre of Partnerships for Development — is a Barcelona-based consultancy working on climate and development with multilateral institutions, governments and European programmes. I joined as a Junior Consultant in March 2026.

## What I did

- **Tender and ToR screening.** I systematised how the team screens public tenders and terms of reference — reading tender documents, extracting requirements, checking technical, financial and eligibility criteria, and tracking opportunities. With an AI-assisted approach, analysis time fell by around 30%. I prepare and lead the weekly commercial meeting, presenting bid/no-bid recommendations to management.
- **Policy analysis.** I produced a comparative analysis of climate-adaptation policies across 20+ Mediterranean countries for the Union for the Mediterranean, and presented findings to intergovernmental committees.
- **European programmes.** I redesigned the monitoring tools of an Interreg MED co-funded programme — adding filters for closed projects and disambiguating funding axes — improving project traceability and milestone tracking.
- **Institutional coordination.** I prepare and take part in meetings with UNICEF and Yemen's Ministry of Water and Environment, documenting requirements, agreements, owners and follow-up actions. I also support planning and monitoring of the PROREV project in Mauritania, funded by the African Development Bank.
- **Data.** I rebuilt, normalised and de-duplicated an institutional database of 2,261 records, and work with datasets of tens of thousands of records in Excel (Power Query, PivotTables, dashboards), Power BI, Stata and R.
- **Training.** I designed and delivered internal training on generative AI and workflow automation, translating new tools into concrete uses for research, data and documentation.

## Impact

- ≈30% less time spent analysing tenders.
- Cleaner, traceable programme-monitoring and institutional data.
- Colleagues adopting AI tools in day-to-day research and documentation.
